02 / A LITTLE CHAT

STAY A LITTLE LONGER.

Some of my favorite moments are the quiet ones.

Two people sitting together, rocking back and forth. One story leads to another; sometimes nobody says anything at all.

I imagined these rocking chairs as a little place for conversations with no agenda — silly thoughts, half-finished stories, and the kind of silence that feels comfortable.

Nothing to rush. Nowhere else to be.

Just take a seat, and stay for a while.
